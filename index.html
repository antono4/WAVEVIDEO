Guide & shortcuts

  1. Load audio — drop a file on the canvas, or open the Audio section.
  2. Pick a preset — thumbnails render with the real engine, so what you see is what you get.
  3. Fine tune — visualizer, backdrop, titles, lyrics and overlay layers.
  4. Export — choose resolution and bitrate, then record. Rendering is realtime.

Timeline

Click anywhere on the waveform to seek. Enable trim in the Audio section to set in and out points; exports honour the trim range. Lyric timestamps show up as markers.

Playing

Press the round button on the preview, the Play button under the timeline, or simply click the artwork itself. Space bar works too.

Lyrics

One line per row as 0:12.5|Line text|Optional translation. Wrap words in *asterisks* for a per-word karaoke fill. Import and export standard .lrc files.
